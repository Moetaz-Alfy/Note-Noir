How It's Composed
  • 01

    Tell us who you are

    A short, personality-driven questionnaire — mood, memory, the way you move through a room.

  • 02

    The AI composes your accord

    Your answers are translated into a note structure: top, heart, and base, matched to thousands of fragrance profiles.

  • 03

    We blend and bottle it

    Perfumers hand-finish the formula and pour a bottle that exists only for you.

  • 04

    It arrives, signed

    Delivered in black glass, engraved with your scent's name — yours to keep, or return to for a refill.
